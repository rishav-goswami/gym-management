Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 September 2026

FitGy, operated by Gym Management, is a fitness platform for adults. It provides a private personal fitness space and, when a user chooses to join a gym, gym membership and operational services. This policy explains how FitGy accesses, collects, uses, shares, retains and protects personal information.

Information we collect

  • Account and profile: Firebase user identifier, name, email address, phone number, authentication provider, age confirmation, date of birth when supplied, gender when supplied, profile image and policy acceptance records.
  • Fitness and wellness: fitness goals, experience, schedule, equipment, height, weight, body measurements, optional limitations or injury notes, routines, workout logs, sets, repetitions, load, cardio activity, personal records and optional progress photos.
  • Gym services: gym affiliation and role, invitations, membership plan and status, externally received payment records and receipts, attendance, class bookings, trainer assignments, announcements and gym support conversations.
  • Support content: messages, categories, status, optional image attachments and audited support-access history.
  • Device and diagnostics: push-notification tokens, App Check integrity signals, app instance and device identifiers, approximate technical information, crash reports, performance diagnostics and privacy-safe feature usage. The app does not use advertising identifiers or sell data for advertising.

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a gym QR code or capture/select an image. We do not use the camera to diagnose, measure or infer a health condition.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, provide personal fitness tracking, generate general non-medical workout suggestions, operate gym memberships and classes, send requested service notifications, provide support, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce quotas, diagnose failures, maintain security and improve aggregate product performance.

Sharing and disclosure

Personal fitness data is private by default. It is projected to a joined gym only after the user grants sharing separately for profile basics, goals, workout summaries, measurements or progress. Revoking a category removes that projection. Authorized gym personnel can access only information allowed by their role and the user's grant.

Gym-authored membership, attendance, payment, class and support records belong to the gym's operational history. We use Google Firebase and Google Cloud as service providers for authentication, database, file storage, notifications, integrity protection, crash reporting, performance monitoring and analytics. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect users and the service. We do not sell personal or health data.

Retention and deletion

Users can request account deletion in Profile → Settings → Delete account or through the web deletion process. Sharing is revoked and sign-in is disabled immediately. Personal Firestore data, personal Storage media and the Firebase Authentication account are scheduled for permanent deletion within 30 days.

Resolved support message content and attachments are scheduled for deletion after 12 months. Limited audit, fraud-prevention or legal records may be retained when reasonably necessary. Gym-authored membership, payment, attendance and other operational records may be retained by the relevant gym for accounting, legal, security and reactivation purposes; they are no longer available as personal fitness data. Service-provider backups may remain isolated until their normal secure rotation expires.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS and encrypted at rest by our cloud providers. Firebase Authentication, App Check with Play Integrity, server-side authorization, tenant-isolated security rules, least-privilege roles, private media paths, bounded access and audited administrative support are used to protect data. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can update profile information, control each gym-sharing category, leave a gym without deleting personal fitness history, export your data, revoke device permissions and request account deletion. You may contact us to ask about access, correction, deletion or privacy concerns.

Adults and health disclaimer

The service is intended for people aged 18 or older. It provides general fitness education and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Stop exercising and seek qualified help for pain, dizziness or unusual discomfort.
