Delete your FitGy account

You can request deletion either inside the app or by email if you no longer have access to it.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to FitGy.
  2. Open Profile, then Settings.
  3. Select Delete account and confirm.

Request deletion on the web

Email us from the address associated with your account. Include “Delete my FitGy account” and your registered email address or phone number. Do not send your password, OTP or identity documents in the first message. We may ask for limited account verification before acting to prevent unauthorized deletion.

Email a deletion request

What happens

  • Access is disabled and personal fitness sharing is revoked when the verified request is accepted.
  • Your authentication account, private personal fitness records, personal support content and personal media are scheduled for permanent deletion within 30 days.
  • Gym-authored membership, payment, attendance, class and audit records may be retained by the gym for accounting, legal, fraud-prevention and reactivation purposes. They are not retained as part of your private personal fitness space.
  • Service-provider backups may remain isolated until normal secure rotation expires.

If you only want to leave a gym but keep your personal fitness account, use Profile → My gym membership → Settings → Leave gym instead.
